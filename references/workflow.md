# Shared Workflow and Output Schemas

## Input summary

Summarize target platform/marketplace/language/category, verified facts and boundaries, variation structure, supplied keyword priorities, buyer questions, current fields, `DATA_MISSING`, `ASSUMPTION` and conflicts. Carry forward upstream conclusions rather than reproducing full research.

## Keyword placement

| Keyword | Upstream role | Relevance | Evidence state | Status | Platform field | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses are `use`, `qualified_use`, `evidence_pending`, `brand_isolate`, and `irrelevant`. Treat upstream priority as input, do not force every term into copy, and map terms only to fields available on the selected platform.

## Buyer-question library

| Priority | Buyer question | Source/evidence | Purchase stage | Answer status | Current verified answer | Best platform field | Evidence needed | Publish status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Answer statuses are `answerable`, `evidence_pending`, and `irrelevant`; publish statuses are `publish` and `hold`.

- Competitors/reviews establish questions, not focal-product answers.
- Every `evidence_pending` question is held and names needed proof.
- Every published question is answerable with a verified answer.
- Route questions into copy, structured attributes, images/rich content, or an internal assistant-intent library according to the selected platform.
- Do not assume a marketplace exposes seller-authored Q&A.

## Claim and expression

| Opportunity/claim | Current evidence | Current status | Allowed wording now | Boundary | Evidence needed | Wording unlocked after proof | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses are `verified`, `observed`, `evidence_pending`, and `prohibited`. Choose a verified expression, a qualified expression, an evidence task/hold, or prohibition.

## Platform-native Listing package

Read the selected platform reference. Across platforms:

- Titles/product names prioritize product clarity, primary generic intent and supported differentiation.
- Longer allowed limits are room for useful information, not a target to fill mechanically.
- Each feature/bullet has one communication job. Avoid an overview followed by repeated component detail; separate fit, organization logic, distinctive feature, use/mobility and trust/specification.
- Descriptions explain role, differentiation, scenarios, specifications and boundaries without repeating every feature.
- Rich-content/image briefs state job, buyer question, message, image concept, proof, mobile crop, dependency and status.
- Conversational coverage uses natural buyer questions; it is not an official readiness score.

## Execution changes

| Priority | Platform field | Current issue | Exact action | Replacement/hold instruction | Dependency | Executable now | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- `P0`: unsupported/high-risk copy, contradictory required fact, trademark/field-rule failure, or category-required missing evidence.
- `P1`: material relevance, conversion, clarity or evidence opportunity that does not block a safe version.
- `P2`: optional wording, layout or experiment.

Separate executable actions, evidence/product decisions and true blockers. Every action names an actual or explicitly conditional platform field.

## Audit dimensions

Audit factual support, claim/trademark risk, intent coverage, question routing, readability/duplication, live limits, cross-field/variation consistency, attribute/asset completeness and action executability. Never claim marketplace approval, ranking lift, conversion lift or shopping-assistant preference from a heuristic.

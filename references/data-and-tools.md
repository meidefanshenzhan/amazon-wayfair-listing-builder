# Input and Tool Protocol

Use only when normalized upstream inputs are unavailable.

| Need | Preferred source |
|---|---|
| Keyword priorities | Target-marketplace keyword/traffic summary |
| Buyer questions | Review/VOC/Q&A summary with source/date |
| Product facts | Product record, drawing, BOM, manual or authenticated marketplace record |
| Claims | Supplier specifications, tests, certifications and care guide |
| Existing fields | Seller/supplier portal export or current Listing |

Preserve source, marketplace, date and sample size. Do not reinterpret metrics.

## Minimum fallback

Resolve platform and focal product; gather enough terms and buyer questions for fit, specification, use, care and trust; record conflicts/gaps; stop when truthful drafting is possible. Do not expand into full ranking, advertising, market-sizing, competitor-landscape or review-frequency analysis.

## Platform sources

- Amazon: ASIN/SKU and authorized Amazon/Seller Central or licensed research sources.
- Wayfair: Wayfair SKU/product URL and authorized supplier-portal exports.
- Do not scrape restricted pages or invent sales, ranking, traffic or conversion metrics.
- Public competitor pages reveal language and visible attributes, not hidden limits or focal-product facts.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Verified product/supply-chain records.
2. Authorized portal records and licensed research.
3. User-provided research summaries.
4. Authoritative policy/certification sources.
5. Public brand materials and buyer language.
6. Explicit assumptions.

Lower evidence never overrides verified facts. Reviews identify questions, not technical performance. In degraded mode, proceed with verified facts, label assumptions, request only blocking evidence, and disclose that no independent research analysis was performed.
